Weekly Tracker FAQs

This FAQ helps participants complete the Weekly Tracker page in the Digital Detox tool. The Weekly Tracker asks for weekly changes and reductions, not your full current footprint. Enter what changed during that specific challenge week compared with your baseline or usual habit.

NOTE: If your weekly numbers do not show reductions or changes, then enter 0 (and not negative values). You are not penalized for this, but be mindful of your digital footprint.

Important timing note

Each weekly tracker entry covers Monday to Sunday for that week. It does not use a rolling 7-day period.

Before you start

  • Compare this week's activity against your baseline or your usual weekly pattern.
  • Use the same devices and same method each week where possible.
  • If an exact number is not available, a reasonable estimate is fine.
  • For reductions, enter the amount reduced during that week's Monday-Sunday period.
  • The page allows updates during the week, but only the latest weekly entry is stored.

Storage Removed (GB)

This is how much data you deleted this week across your devices or cloud storage, such as files, photos, apps, or old mail. Example: If your phone storage drops from 500 GB to 497 GB and you also delete 2 GB from cloud storage, enter 5.

How to calculate it

  • Check your current storage used.
  • Compare it with your baseline or your previous week's number.
  • Enter the amount deleted during Monday to Sunday of that week.

Android

  • Phone: Settings > Storage
  • Cloud apps: Google Drive / Google One / OneDrive / Dropbox > Storage

iPhone

  • Phone: Settings > General > iPhone Storage
  • Cloud: Settings > Apple ID > iCloud, or your cloud app's Storage page

Windows

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap System.
  3. Tap Storage.
  4. Open the main drive, usually C:.
  5. Compare current used storage with your baseline or last week's number.

Mac

  1. Click the Apple menu.
  2. Click About This Mac.
  3. Open Storage.
  4. Compare current used storage with your baseline or last week's number.

Estimated Downloads Avoided (GB)

This is the amount of downloading you did not do this week compared with a normal week, such as apps, videos, files, or software updates. Example: If you usually download 12 GB in a week but this week you downloaded only 4 GB, enter 8 GB avoided.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Network & Internet, Connections, or SIM & Network.
  3. Tap Data usage or Mobile data usage.
  4. Review the usage for that week where possible.
  5. Compare it with a normal week and estimate what was avoided.

iPhone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Mobile Data or Cellular.
  3. Review the relevant weekly usage if available, or estimate from your recent pattern.
  4. Estimate how much less you downloaded than usual that week.

Windows

  1. Check browser download history, for example Chrome > Downloads or Edge > Downloads.
  2. Review large files, app downloads, media, or software updates for that week.
  3. If available, check Settings > Network & Internet > Data usage.

Mac

  1. Check browser download history, for example Safari > Downloads or Chrome > Downloads.
  2. Review large files, app downloads, media, or software updates for that week.
  3. If you use Activity Monitor or another network tool, use it as a rough guide.

Screen Time Reduction (minutes)

Enter how many minutes less screen time you had this week compared with your baseline. The weekly tracker asks for reduction in minutes, not hours.

Example: If your baseline is 100 minutes per day and this week it is 85 minutes per day, enter 15 minutes.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Digital Wellbeing & parental controls.
  3. Tap the usage chart.
  4. Review the relevant week.
  5. Note your current daily average.
  6. Subtract it from your baseline daily average.

iPhone

On iPhone, open Settings > Screen Time > See All App & Website Activity > Week. Use the Monday-Sunday weekly summary for the challenge week, not a rolling 7-day period.

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Screen Time.
  3. Tap See All App & Website Activity.
  4. Review the relevant week.
  5. Note your current Daily Average.
  6. Subtract it from your baseline daily average.

Windows

  1. If you use Microsoft Family Safety, RescueTime, Clockify, or another time-tracking tool, review daily device usage for that week.
  2. If not, estimate based on your work and leisure usage that week.

Mac

  1. Open System Settings.
  2. Tap Screen Time.
  3. Review usage for the relevant week.
  4. Note your current daily average.

Streaming Time Reduction (minutes)

Enter how many minutes less you spent on streaming this week compared with your baseline or usual week. Example: If your baseline is 60 minutes a day and this week you watched 30 minutes a day, enter 30 minutes.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Digital Wellbeing & parental controls.
  3. Tap the usage chart.
  4. Review the relevant week.
  5. Add the time spent in streaming apps.

iPhone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Screen Time.
  3. Tap See All App & Website Activity.
  4. Review the relevant week.
  5. Add up the time spent on streaming apps.

Windows

  1. Review streaming usage from browser history or streaming app history for that week.
  2. Add up time spent on YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, or other streaming services.

Mac

  1. Open System Settings > Screen Time if you use Screen Time on Mac, or review browser/app history.
  2. Add up your streaming time for that week.

Fewer prompts/questions this week (vs your usual)

Enter how many fewer prompts or follow-up questions you asked AI tools this week compared with your usual weekly pattern. Example: If you usually ask 12 prompts a week and this week you asked 7, enter 5.

How to estimate

  • Think about your normal weekly AI usage from baseline.
  • Estimate how many prompts you sent this week.
  • Subtract this week's number from your baseline number.

Completed Digital Detox Ritual

Tick Yes if you completed the challenge ritual for that week. This is a self-check and does not come from a device setting.

Community Engagement (Interactions)

Enter the number of meaningful challenge-related interactions you had that week. This may include encouraging another participant, sharing progress, inviting someone to join, or joining a challenge-related discussion.

Example: If you encouraged 2 people in the group and invited 1 new person, enter 3 interactions.
