Baseline measurement FAQ

Step-by-step where to find each baseline figure on Android and iPhone. Search by keyword — e.g. iCloud, Digital Wellbeing, Gmail, Screen Time.

Android · iPhone

Android

Google & OEMs

Phone storage usage (GB)

Most Android phones

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Storage (sometimes under Device care / Device maintenance or About phone → Storage).
  3. Look for “Used” / “Used space” or “Internal storage used” in GB.
  4. If there is an SD card, scroll to “Portable storage / SD card” and note used storage (GB) separately.

“On your Android phone, go to Settings → Storage and note the GB used (and SD card used, if any).”

Laptop storage usage (GB)

Measured on the laptop, not the phone.

  • Windows: Settings → System → Storage → “C: drive” → “Used space (GB)”.
  • macOS: Apple menu → About This Mac → Storage → note “Used” in GB.

“On your laptop (Windows/macOS), open storage settings and record how many GB are used on your main drive.”

Cloud storage usage (GB)

Google Drive / Google One

  1. Open the Google Drive or Google One app.
  2. Tap your profile icon (top right).
  3. Tap Storage or Storage used.
  4. Note “X GB of Y GB used”.

Microsoft OneDrive

  1. Open OneDrive.
  2. Tap Me (bottom right) or profile icon.
  3. Look for Storage or X GB used.

Dropbox

  1. Open Dropbox.
  2. Tap Menu / ☰ or profile icon.
  3. Look for “Space used”.

“Check the storage screen in the app you mainly use (Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud/Files, Dropbox) and note GB used.”

Email mailbox size (GB)

Web or account storage pages are usually easiest on Android.

Google Account (Gmail)

  1. In Chrome on your phone, go to one.google.com/storage (or search “Google storage”).
  2. Sign in if needed.
  3. You’ll see Gmail / Drive / Photos breakdown.
  4. Use the Gmail number as your mailbox size (GB).

Other providers (Outlook, Yahoo, etc.)

  1. In Chrome, open Outlook.com (or your provider’s webmail).
  2. Open Settings / Options.
  3. Look for Storage / Mailbox usage / Mailbox size.
  4. Note the used storage in GB.

“Open your email provider’s web view on your phone and look for ‘storage’ or ‘mailbox usage’; note the GB used.”

Daily screen time (hours per day)

Digital Wellbeing

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Digital Wellbeing & parental controls (or Digital Wellbeing).
  3. Tap the chart / dashboard at the top.
  4. Switch to “Last 7 days” view (if available).
  5. Note the average screen time per day (e.g. “5 h 12 m”).

Some OEMs (e.g. Samsung)

  1. Settings → Digital Wellbeing and parental controls or Device care → Screen time.
  2. Look for “Daily average” for the last 7 days.

“Use Digital Wellbeing (Settings → Digital Wellbeing) and write down your ‘daily average’ screen time over the last 7 days.”

Streaming consumption (hours per week)

There is no single “streaming only” total — use app timers.

  1. Open Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls.
  2. Tap the usage chart and view “Last 7 days”.
  3. Look at time spent in video/streaming apps (e.g. YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, etc.).
  4. Add up minutes/hours for those apps over the last 7 days.
  5. Convert to hours per week (e.g. 420 minutes ≈ 7 hours).

Social apps (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube)

The baseline form does not ask for minutes per day on individual social apps. Use average daily screen time and streaming hours per week to reflect your overall media use; those fields cover YouTube and similar video time at a high level.

Mobile data usage (downloads / week, GB)

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Network & Internet (or Connections / SIM & Network, depending on brand).
  3. Tap Data usage / Mobile data usage.
  4. Choose the current billing cycle or set the date range to the last 7 days (if supported).
  5. You’ll see apps listed with data in MB / GB for that period.
  6. To approximate “data downloaded per week (GB)”: use total mobile data for the last 7 days, or add heavy-download apps (YouTube, Netflix, Play Store, games). Convert MB → GB (1,000 MB ≈ 1 GB).

“Go to Settings → Network & Internet (or Connections) → Data usage → Mobile data usage. Set it to the last 7 days and use the total data (in GB) as your weekly ‘data downloaded’ baseline.”

iPhone

iOS

Phone storage usage (GB)

iOS 15–17 and similar

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap General.
  3. Tap iPhone Storage.
  4. At the top, note “Used” or the coloured bar (e.g. “64 GB of 128 GB used”).

“On your iPhone, go to Settings → General → iPhone Storage and write down how many GB are used.”

Laptop storage usage (GB)

Same idea as Android — measure on the laptop.

  • Mac: Apple menu → About This Mac → Storage → “Used” in GB.
  • Windows: Settings → System → Storage → main drive → “Used space (GB)”.

“On your laptop (Mac/Windows), open storage settings and record how many GB are used on your main drive.”

Cloud storage usage (GB)

iCloud

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your Apple ID name at the top.
  3. Tap iCloud.
  4. Tap iCloud Storage or view the storage bar.
  5. Note “X GB of Y GB used”.

Google Drive

  1. Open Google Drive.
  2. Tap profile picture (top right) → Storage; note GB used.

OneDrive

  1. Open OneDrive → Me or profile → Storage / “X GB used”.

Dropbox

  1. Open Dropbox → Account or profile → “Space used”.

“On iPhone, check iCloud storage (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → iCloud Storage) and/or your main cloud app (Drive/OneDrive/Dropbox) and note the GB used.”

Email mailbox size (GB)

The Mail app has no single mailbox-size screen — use the provider’s web or account page.

Gmail (Safari / Chrome)

  1. Open Safari or Chrome.
  2. Go to one.google.com/storage.
  3. Sign in if needed.
  4. Note Gmail storage used (e.g. “Gmail – 4.2 GB”).

Outlook / others

  1. Open Outlook.com (or Yahoo / other webmail) in Safari/Chrome.
  2. Settings → e.g. View all Outlook settings → General → Storage (wording varies).
  3. Note mailbox used in GB.

“On your iPhone, open your email provider in Safari (e.g. Outlook.com or one.google.com/storage for Gmail) and look for ‘Storage’ or ‘Mailbox usage’; write down the GB used.”

Daily screen time (hours per day)

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Screen Time.
  3. If Screen Time is off, tap Turn On Screen Time and let it run for a week before relying on averages.
  4. On the main Screen Time page, note the “Daily Average” (e.g. “4h 35m”).

“On your iPhone, go to Settings → Screen Time and note the ‘Daily Average’ screen time (hours and minutes).”

Streaming consumption (hours per week)

Use Screen Time’s 7-day app breakdown.

  1. Settings → Screen Time.
  2. Tap “See All App & Website Activity” (or the weekly chart).
  3. Set view to “Last 7 Days” at the top.
  4. Under Most Used, find video/streaming apps (YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV, etc.).
  5. Add time for those apps over the last 7 days; convert to hours per week for your baseline.

Social apps (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube)

The baseline form does not ask for minutes per day on individual social apps. Use Screen Time daily average and streaming hours per week instead.

Mobile data usage (downloads / week, GB)

  1. Settings → Mobile Data (or Cellular).
  2. Scroll the app list; at the top note Current Period total in GB (since last reset).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and check “Last Reset” date.
  4. Best for a clean weekly number: at the start of your challenge week, tap Reset Statistics. After 7 days, return and use Current Period in GB as your weekly baseline.
  5. To approximate now: days since last reset → divide total GB by that number, then multiply by 7 for an approximate weekly GB.

“On iPhone, go to Settings → Mobile Data. Scroll to the bottom and tap ‘Reset Statistics’ at the start of the week. After 7 days, return and use the ‘Current Period’ number in GB as your weekly data downloaded baseline.”
