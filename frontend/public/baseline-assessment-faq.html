Baseline FAQs

This FAQ helps participants complete the Baseline Assessment page in the Digital Detox tool. The baseline is your starting point and should reflect your current digital habits before the challenge begins.

Before you start

  • Complete the baseline once only.
  • If you need to redo the baseline, email system admin at digital-detox@communityimpactchallenge.org.
  • Use the same devices and accounts whenever possible so later comparisons stay meaningful.
  • If an exact number is not available, a reasonable estimate is fine.
  • If a field does not apply, enter 0.

Phones Owned

Enter the number of smartphones you actively use. Do not count broken, old, or unused phones.

Example: If you use one personal phone and one work phone regularly, enter 2.

Storage Used on Phone (GB)

Enter the approximate storage currently used across your phone(s). Example: If Phone A shows 220 GB used and Phone B shows 280 GB used, enter 500.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Storage.
  3. On some phones, this may be under Device care, Device maintenance, or About phone > Storage.
  4. Note the Used or Used space amount in GB.

iPhone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap General.
  3. Tap iPhone Storage.
  4. Note the used amount shown at the top.

Tablets Owned

Enter the number of tablets you actively use.

Example: If you do not use any tablet, enter 0.

Storage Used on Tablet (GB)

Enter the approximate storage currently used across your tablet(s). Example: If you have no tablet, enter 0. If you use one tablet showing 48 GB used, enter 48.

Android tablet

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Storage.
  3. Note the used storage in GB.

iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap General.
  3. Tap iPad Storage.
  4. Note the used storage in GB.

Computers Owned (Laptops/Desktops)

Enter the number of laptops or desktops you use regularly.

Example: If you use one work laptop only, enter 1.

Storage Used on Computer (GB)

Enter the approximate storage currently used across your computer(s). Example: If your laptop shows 1000 GB used, enter 1000.

Windows

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap System.
  3. Tap Storage.
  4. Open the main drive, usually C:.
  5. Note the Used space in GB.

Mac

  1. Click the Apple menu.
  2. Click About This Mac.
  3. Open Storage.
  4. Note the Used amount in GB.

Active Cloud Storage Accounts

Enter the number of cloud services you actively use, such as Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive, or Dropbox.

Example: If you use Google Drive, iCloud, and Dropbox, enter 3.

Cloud Storage Used (GB)

Add together the storage used across your active cloud accounts. Example: If Google Drive = 800 GB, iCloud = 900 GB, and Dropbox = 300 GB, enter 2000.

Android - Google Drive / Google One

  1. Open Google Drive or Google One.
  2. Tap your profile icon.
  3. Tap Storage or Storage used.
  4. Note the GB used.

Android - OneDrive

  1. Open OneDrive.
  2. Tap Me or your profile icon.
  3. Note the storage used.

Android - Dropbox

  1. Open Dropbox.
  2. Tap Menu, Account, or your profile icon.
  3. Look for Space used.

iPhone - iCloud

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name / Apple ID.
  3. Tap iCloud.
  4. Note the storage bar or iCloud Storage number.

iPhone - Google Drive / Google One

  1. Open Google Drive or Google One.
  2. Tap your profile icon.
  3. Tap Storage.
  4. Note the GB used.

iPhone - OneDrive / Dropbox

Use the same approach in the app and add the numbers across services.

Windows / Mac

You can also check the storage pages from web or desktop apps for Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or iCloud and total them manually.

Total Time Spent on Screens per DAY (phone, computer, tablet combined)

Enter your average weekly screen time across the devices you use. The baseline page asks for combined screen time, not phone-only screen time.

Example: If your phone averages 2.5 hours per day and your laptop averages 1.5 hours per day, enter 4.

Android phone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Digital Wellbeing & parental controls.
  3. Tap the chart/dashboard.
  4. Use Last 7 days if available.
  5. Note the daily average screen time.

iPhone

On iPhone, open Settings > Screen Time > See All App & Website Activity. If this is your first time, turn on App & Website Activity so Screen Time can track your usage. Use the Weekly view to see your current average screen time.

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Screen Time.
  3. Tap See All App & Website Activity if needed.
  4. Use Last 7 Days.
  5. Note the Daily Average.

Windows

Use Microsoft Family Safety, RescueTime, Clockify, or your own reasonable estimate if you spend significant time on your laptop/desktop.

Mac

  1. Open System Settings.
  2. Tap Screen Time.
  3. Review your daily average.

Video Streaming (hours/WEEK)

Enter the total time spent watching streaming content over a typical week. Example: If you watch about 2 hours on weekdays and 5 hours across the weekend, enter 15 hours/week.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Digital Wellbeing & parental controls.
  3. Tap the usage chart.
  4. Switch to Last 7 days.
  5. Add up time spent in streaming apps such as YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, or similar.

iPhone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Screen Time.
  3. Tap See All App & Website Activity.
  4. Choose Last 7 Days.
  5. Under Most Used, add the time spent on streaming apps.

Windows / Mac

Use browser history, streaming app history, Screen Time on Mac, or a reasonable estimate based on your normal weekly watching.

Number of prompts/questions asked per WEEK to GenAI

Estimate how many prompts or follow-up messages you send in a typical week across all AI tools you use. Example: If you ask AI tools 8 prompts a day for 5 days, enter 40.

Include tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, or Copilot.

Estimated Weekly Downloads (GB)

This is an estimate of data downloaded weekly, including apps, files, offline content, videos, and updates. Estimated values are fine. Example: If you download about 10 GB of videos, 30 GB of work files, and 10 GB of apps/updates in a week, enter 50.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Network & Internet, Connections, or SIM & Network.
  3. Tap Data usage or Mobile data usage.
  4. Set the range to last 7 days if available.
  5. Use the total shown in GB as your weekly estimate.

iPhone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Mobile Data or Cellular.
  3. Review the Current Period usage.
  4. If statistics were recently reset, use that as your guide.
  5. If not, estimate a weekly average from recent usage.

Windows

  1. Check browser download history, for example Chrome > Downloads or Edge > Downloads.
  2. Review large files, app downloads, media, or software updates from a typical week.
  3. If available, check Settings > Network & Internet > Data usage.

Mac

  1. Check browser download history, for example Safari > Downloads or Chrome > Downloads.
  2. Review large files, app downloads, media, or software updates from a typical week.
  3. If you use Activity Monitor or another network tracking tool, use it as a rough guide.
